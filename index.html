About Me

I am Qingqing Jiang, a PhD student at University of Münster. My primary research interests include geology, paleoclimatology, and astronomical chronology, with a focus on the orbital forcing on the early Eocene climate-carbon cycle. My master project most focused on the Paleocene-Eocene Thermal Maximum (PETM). And the method I used is most time series analysis to build the astronomical time scale, and finding the mechanisms of PETM. My PhD project is most about exploring Earth system models to understand the orbital forcing on climate systems.

Research

My research focuses on the early Eocene climate, investigating its climatic and sedimentological impacts using XRF analysis and astronomical chronology. And also orbital forcing on the early Eocene climate-carbon cycles.

  • Sedimentation rates and orbital forcing during the PETM.
  • Earth system modeling using cGENIE to simulate climate impacts.
